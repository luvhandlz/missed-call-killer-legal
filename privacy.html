Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 2026

Missed Call Killer ("we," "us") provides an automated text-message follow-up service on behalf of local home-services businesses ("Business Clients"). This policy explains how we handle information when you text or call a phone number operated by Missed Call Killer on behalf of a Business Client.

Information We Collect

When you call or text a Missed Call Killer-operated number, we collect your phone number, the content of your messages, and any details you share (such as your name, service needed, or address) in order to respond to your inquiry.

How We Use Information

We use this information solely to: reply to your missed call, understand what service you need, schedule an appointment with the Business Client, and pass your request along to the Business Client. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Sharing

We share your information with the Business Client you contacted (so they can complete your service request) and with our service providers (Twilio for messaging, Anthropic for conversational processing, Google for calendar scheduling) solely to operate this service. We do not share your information with unrelated third parties.

Message Frequency & Opt-Out

Message frequency varies based on your conversation. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages, or HELP for assistance.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to lincolnhendry@icloud.com.
