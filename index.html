Questions

The stuff owners ask us.

Do I have to change my phone number?

No. You keep your current number and answer calls exactly like you do now. We only step in when a call goes unanswered.

Is this hard to set up?

Not for you — we handle the whole setup. Once it's running, there's nothing new for you to learn or manage.

Will customers know it's automated?

It sounds like a friendly member of your team. If someone directly asks whether they're texting a real person, it answers honestly — but it always represents your business professionally, and you can jump into any conversation yourself anytime.

What if I want to take over a conversation?

You can, anytime. If a customer needs you specifically — or asks for a real person — you get a text immediately, and you reply straight from your own phone to talk to them directly. No new app, no separate number to manage.

How do I know it's actually working?

You get your own private dashboard — every lead, every booking, every full conversation, live, in one link you bookmark. Plus a text the moment a job books, so you're never wondering what's happening behind the scenes.

Does it help with reviews?

Yes — once a job's marked done, it automatically texts the customer asking for a Google or Yelp review. More reviews without you having to remember to ask.

What kinds of businesses is this for?

Any local service business that lives and dies by the phone — HVAC, plumbing, concrete and epoxy, electrical, and more. If a missed call means a missed job, this is for you.

Is this just for missed calls, or more than that?

Missed-call text-back is how most owners meet Reclaim, but it's the front door to the whole platform — job scheduling, crew management, estimates & invoicing, memberships, review requests, and reporting all live in the same system. See the full feature list.
