Questions

Pricing, answered.

Is there a setup fee?

No. We set up your dedicated business line, wire it to your Google Calendar, tune the conversation for your trade, and test it end-to-end — all included, free. You don't lift a finger.

Is there a long-term contract?

No. It's month-to-month. If it's not catching you jobs, you can cancel — we're confident it will.

How fast can I be up and running?

Usually within a few days of getting started. Setup on our end is quick once we have your number and calendar access.

What if I get more calls than my plan covers?

Good problem to have. We'll just move you up to the plan that fits your volume — no penalty, no drama.

Which plan should I choose?

Most shops land on Growth. If you're a solo operator, Starter is plenty; if you're catching a high volume of calls or running multiple crews, Pro removes the cap. Running 10+ lines or multiple locations? Reach out about a custom Industrial quote instead.
