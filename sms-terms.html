SMS Terms & Opt-In (Call-to-Action)

Effective date: August 2026

Reclaim provides an automated text-message reply service on behalf of local home-services businesses ("Business Clients"). This page describes the SMS program, how consumers opt in, and how to opt out.

How you opt in (Call-to-Action)

The consumer initiates all contact. When you call the published phone number of a business served by Reclaim and your call is not answered, you will receive a single automated text-message reply — on behalf of that business — to the mobile number you called from, so the conversation you started can continue. By placing that call, you provide your mobile number and consent to receive this reply and follow-up messages related to your inquiry.

We only send messages in reply to a consumer's own inbound call to a Business Client. We do not text numbers obtained from any third party, purchased list, or affiliate, and we never message anyone who has not first contacted the business.

Example of the first message

"Hey! This is [Business Name] — sorry we missed your call. What can we help you with today? Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. Msg & data rates may apply."

Types of messages

Messages are conversational and relate solely to your inquiry: understanding the service you need, answering basic questions, and scheduling an appointment with the Business Client.

Message frequency

Message frequency varies based on your conversation. Messages are only sent in response to your own call or replies.

Cost

Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier plan. Reclaim does not charge you for these messages.

Opt-out and help

Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for help. Opt-out requests are honored automatically.

Privacy

No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. See our Privacy Policy for full details, including our SMS Consent Notice.

Contact

Questions about this SMS program can be directed through missedcallkiller.com.
